Baud:

1200 baud uses ordinary FM speaker audio (1200/2200 Hz tones). 9600 baud requires flat discriminator/DATA audio at 48 kHz. Keep squelch open and disable speech processing. Receive only on RF; optional live APRS-IS forwarding is configured below.

APRS-IS upload settings

Forward newly received radio packets to APRS-IS. File decoding and SDR demo/replay never upload. No radio transmission is performed. Your QTH position is sent after login and every 30 minutes while live uploading is enabled.
