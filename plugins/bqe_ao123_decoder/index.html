Use the BQE SDR's BPSK mode at 435.400 MHz, 16000 Hz bandwidth, producing a 12 kHz carrier in 48000 Hz audio. External input must preserve the whole BPSK signal (wide linear audio or baseband stereo I/Q). Ordinary FM voice audio, 3 kHz SSB audio and MP3 are unsuitable.
